# Loan Approval & Risk Analysis — Key Findings

## 1. Objective

* Analyze loan approval patterns.
* Identify factors associated with loan approval and default risk.
* Understand the relationship between customer financial health, credit profile, and approval outcomes.
* Generate business recommendations from the analysis.

---

## 2. Results / Key Findings

* Higher **credit scores** were associated with higher approval rates.
* Higher **income** was associated with better approval outcomes.
* Applicants with better **previous loan repayment history** had higher approval rates.
* Higher **default risk** and more **late payments** were associated with lower approval rates.
* Lower **debt-to-income ratios** were associated with higher approval rates.
* Higher **savings balances** were associated with stronger approval outcomes.
* Applications with **collateral** generally had higher approval rates.
* **South** had the highest application volume, while **West** had the highest average requested loan amount.
* **Online** received the highest number of applications, while **Mobile App** had the highest approval rate.
* Higher-risk applicants generally received **higher interest rates**.
* Lower credit scores were associated with **higher interest rates**.

---

## 3. Business Suggestions / Recommendations

* Use **credit score and repayment history** as important risk indicators.
* Closely evaluate applicants with **high DTI ratios, late payments, or high default risk**.
* Consider **collateral** when evaluating higher-risk applications.
* Use **risk-based pricing** to align interest rates with applicant risk.
* Monitor differences in approval performance across **regions and application channels**.
* Extend the analysis into a **loan approval and default prediction model** using machine learning.

---

## 4. Conclusion

The analysis shows that **credit profile, income, repayment history, debt level, savings, collateral, and risk level** are important factors associated with loan approval and interest-rate patterns.
